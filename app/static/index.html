Upload a CT scan

The system converts the scan to a 3-D volume, extracts the lungs (also from full-body CT), finds nodules, turns each one into a row of measurements and runs XGBoost to estimate whether it is a lung-cancer nodule. You get a report a doctor can act on.

🔒 Please upload anonymised scans only. Do not upload scans that contain patient names or IDs. Uploaded scans and results are deleted automatically after 2 hours. Research prototype, not a medical device.
